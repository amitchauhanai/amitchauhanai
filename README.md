#  About Me:
I am Amit Chauhan, a Computer Science and Engineering student specializing in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Lucknow, with a strong interest in AI/ML, computer vision, remote sensing, and autonomous robotics. I am passionate about developing intelligent systems that combine machine learning, deep learning, geospatial intelligence, and real-time automation to solve real-world problems.<br><br>My experience includes working on AI-driven satellite image analysis, road-scene detection and segmentation, autonomous robotics, multimodal AI agents, and disaster-response drone systems. I have gained practical experience through internships at the Remote Sensing Applications Centre (associated with ISRO), DefendAir Technologies, and NIELIT Gorakhpur.<br><br>I have developed projects such as GeoPath for real-time road-scene monitoring, Drishti for satellite image segmentation, Hey Sai for voice-controlled AI automation, Qubo for quantum circuit simulation, and TARA for AI-powered disaster response. My technical toolkit includes Python, C++, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, YOLO, PyTorch, FastAPI, ROS/ROS2, and embedded systems.<br><br>Beyond development, I have contributed to research and technical competitions, including an ISRO Robotics Challenge qualification-round shortlist, a conference publication on halo coronal mass ejections using Aditya-L1 payload data, and a winning project at the Hack4Good ElevenLabs Track. As a former Head of the LIFE ROBO Robotics Club at the University of Lucknow, I have also led student teams and supported hands-on technical learning.<br><br>My long-term goal is to advance research and development in artificial intelligence, Earth observation, space technology, and intelligent autonomous systems, translating innovative research into practical, impactful solutions.
